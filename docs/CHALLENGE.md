# Hacktoberfest 2026 Week 4 readiness

Theme: Ghost in the Machine. Ghostlight gives fictional forgotten objects a spooky/tender afterlife using real open-weight local AI. It is not a memorial chatbot and never claims to speak for a real deceased person.

Official sources checked October 27, 2026:

- https://dev.to/challenges/hacktoberfest-week4-2026-10-26
- https://dev.to/page/hacktoberfest-week4-2026-10-26-contest-rules

Formal window: October 26, 2026, 9 AM PDT through October 31, 2026, 11:59 PM PDT. Eastern conversion: October 26 noon EDT through November 1 at the second 1:59 AM EST (06:59 UTC).

The project and local repository were newly created during this window. It does not reuse a prior challenge repository. Public repository creation, publication, license approval and contest submission remain separate actions, subject to owner approval.

Required before entry:

- [x] New project responding to theme.
- [x] Open-weight model central to real generation, not a sample-only façade.
- [ ] Real browser inference verification complete (see VERIFICATION).
- [ ] Owner-approved application license and public repository.
- [ ] Public demo link or video.
- [ ] DEV submission template post with `devchallenge` and `hf26challenge` tags.
- [ ] Explain model, architecture, local-data benefit and actual limitations.
- [ ] Disclose AI assistance accurately.
- [ ] Owner reviews entry and applicable contest terms before submission.
- [ ] Verify receipt after authorized submission.

One entry per participant. Partner technology and agent-session sharing are optional; no paid service or partner claim is necessary. Writing quality is weighted most heavily. Do not claim prize eligibility, a badge, dashboard sync or submission from a local build.
